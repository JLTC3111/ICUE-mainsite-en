Cookie Policy

Understand cookies and how we use them to improve your experience

Cookies – What Are They?

Cookies are small files stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help the website "remember" information about your visit, making your next visit easier and more useful.
🍪 Cookies cannot harm your device. They cannot contain viruses, malware, or access your personal files.
At ICUE, we use cookies to improve your experience on our website, analyze usage, and provide better services.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use several different types of cookies, each with its own purpose:

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled.
  • Purpose: Security, login, shopping cart, language preferences
  • Duration: Session or up to 1 year
  • Examples: PHPSESSID, language_preference, security_token
  • Can be disabled: ❌ No

2. Performance Cookies

Collect information about how you use the website to help us improve it.
  • Purpose: Traffic analysis, page performance, technical errors
  • Duration: 1–2 years
  • Examples: Google Analytics, heatmap tracking
  • Can be disabled: ✅ Yes

3. Functional Cookies

Remember your choices to provide a personalized experience.
  • Purpose: Interface preferences, geolocation, personal settings
  • Duration: 6 months – 2 years
  • Examples: theme_preference, location_setting
  • Can be disabled: ✅ Yes

4. Marketing Cookies

Used to track visitors across websites and display relevant ads.
  • Purpose: Targeted ads, campaign effectiveness measurement
  • Duration: 1 month – 2 years
  • Examples: Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, retargeting
  • Can be disabled: ✅ Yes

How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

You can manage cookies directly through your browser settings:

Browser Guide:

Chrome: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and other site data
Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
💡 Tip: Most modern browsers allow you to block third-party cookies while still allowing essential ones.
You can also:
  • Use private/incognito browsing mode
  • Install ad blocker and tracker blocker extensions
  • Delete all cookies when closing the browser
  • Set whitelist/blacklist for specific websites
